# 542 night campaign — frame-rate baro edge (ORB_BARO_FRAME_SIGMA): built, works, neutral

**Date:** 2026-07-16 ~04:45 (autonomous night run, batch 5)

## Motivation

The 10 s-window drift metric samples **per-frame** poses, but EdgeBaroZ only constrains
**keyframes** (LocalInertialBA / FIBA). Between KFs the z of tracked frames is vision+IMU only.
Hypothesis: fusing baro at frame rate (like a real EKF) removes the inter-KF drift component.

## Implementation (ORB_SLAM3 fork, `src/Optimizer.cc`)

`AddFrameBaroEdge(...)` — file-local helper called from **both** tracking-thread inertial pose
optimizers (`PoseInertialOptimizationLastKeyFrame` + `...LastFrame`), right after the `EdgeAccRW`
block:

- unary `EdgeBaroZ` on the frame's `VertexPose`;
- measurement = `z_ref + (baro(t_frame) − baro(t_ref))` where ref = last KF (resp. previous
  frame) — **relative datum, causal, no absolute-height coupling**;
- quadratic (no Huber, same rationale as the BA edges), skipped when the predicted residual
  exceeds `ORB_BARO_GATE` (breakage guard);
- env `ORB_BARO_FRAME_SIGMA` [m]; **0/unset = stock behaviour** (flag-gated like everything else).

No crashes, no tracking regressions at σ_f ≥ 0.5 (full airborne coverage in 5/6 runs).

## Results on top of the σ0.075 recipe (GT = real baro, same-span NORA bar: vATE 1.42 / RMS 1.44)

| arm | vATE draws | drift RMS draws |
|---|---|---|
| f σ0.5 ×3 | 1.04 · 1.97 · 1.00 | 1.30 · 1.59 · 1.73 |
| f σ0.25 ×3 | 1.46 · 2.51 · 2.75 | **0.87** · 2.19 · 1.87 |
| f σ1.0 ×2 | 1.28 · 2.27 | 2.99 · 1.71 |
| (no frame edge, batch 3-4, ×8) | median 1.11 | median 1.53 |

Verdict: **neutral at σ_f=0.5, harmful tighter/looser.** The remaining window drift is not
inter-KF z wander — it is (a) draw-to-draw map noise and (b) the barometer's own dynamic error
in the landing window (138-148 s: every estimator including NORA shows −2.3…−3.6 m there —
ground-effect pressure transient). Frame-rate fusion cannot fix either. Keeping the code
(useful, cheap, flag-gated); final recipe decision goes to batch 6 medians
(σ0.075 plain n=14 vs σ0.075+f0.5 n=9).
